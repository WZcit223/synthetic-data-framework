What happens if we take an action? Each intervention is compared with the baseline on paired replicate worlds of the dashboard's current world: every pair shares a seed, and the effect is the mean of the paired differences, with a confidence interval. An interval that covers 0 means the simulation cannot tell the effect apart from no effect.

Observed data does not come with its effects. An estimator infers one from rows, adjusting for the covariates you declare; if the set misses a cause of both treatment and outcome, the estimate is biased. The promotion benchmark knows its true effect: high-demand SKUs are promoted more often, so the naive difference is confounded. Compare the estimators with the truth, drop covariates to watch the bias return, and sweep the confounding.
